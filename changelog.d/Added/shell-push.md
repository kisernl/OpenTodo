- The Android app can receive reminders by push: with a UnifiedPush app such as ntfy on the phone, **Settings → Notifications → Deliver instantly through UnifiedPush** subscribes the app on your server like a browser, so a reminder set on another device a minute ago arrives on time with the app closed, without Google services. Off until turned on; the push service sees only an encrypted message, and the reminders scheduled on the phone keep working either way (shell-push, #1344).
